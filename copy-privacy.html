Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 31, 2026

1. Information Collected

The Bot may store the following information:

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Guild IDs
  • Channel IDs
  • Role IDs
  • Server backup data
  • Configuration settings
  • Audit and log information

2. Purpose of Data Collection

The collected information is used solely for:

  • Providing bot functionality
  • Server backup and restore operations
  • Permission management
  • Logging and troubleshooting
  • Bot administration

3. Data Sharing

The Bot does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

4. Data Retention

Stored data may remain until:

  • Deleted by the user
  • Deleted by the server owner
  • Removed during maintenance

5. Security

Reasonable efforts are made to protect stored data, but no method of storage is completely secure.

6. User Rights

Server owners may request deletion of stored configuration data related to their server.

7. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time. Continued use of the Bot constitutes acceptance of any updates.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the Bot developer through Discord.
